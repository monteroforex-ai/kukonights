Terminal Zero

Latin nightclub · day parties and night

Terminal Zero is the home of day parties and latin nights in El Prat de Llobregat. There's music by day and by night, with plenty of old school reggaeton, and the best part is you don't have to head into Barcelona to have a great time. So you can come without worrying about a thing, at KuKo events we organise a taxi and pick you up.

Frequently asked questions

What does KuKo run at Terminal Zero?

Day parties and latin nights, with old school reggaeton included. Check the agenda for each date and the opening hours.

Is there a taxi to get there?

Yes. At KuKo events we organise a taxi so you get there with zero hassle, without travelling on your own.

What is the minimum age?

Entry is for over 21s only. Bring your ID or passport.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual. Avoid very informal sportswear.

Can I book a table or bottle service?

Yes, tables and bottle service are available subject to availability. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out.

Where do I buy tickets?

On our Fourvenues agenda. You can also get on the guestlist on WhatsApp.

Coming to the next day party?

Old school reggaeton, latin nights and a KuKo taxi so you arrive hassle-free. Enjoy the party in El Prat, with no trip into Barcelona, and save your spot.
