About KuKo

Barcelona sets the stage. You bring the attitude.

Nightlife with a different style. Ours.

KuKo Nights is a Barcelona nightlife brand that blends music, style and experiences for people who love going out and living the night a little differently. From reggaeton, bachata and old school to our Ladies Nights and day parties, we create plans where music, fashion and atmosphere come together to make every night out something special.

Nights that leave a mark

We're into looks that stand out, people with personality, and the moments that start as a plan and end up as memories. We want every event to have its own essence: come with your friends, meet new people and, by the time the night ends, already be thinking about the next one.

Your next plan starts here

Discover our upcoming events in Barcelona and nearby, find your vibe and get ready to live the night with KuKo.

Come for the plan. Stay for the experience. Come back for KuKo.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is KuKo Nights?
A nightlife brand from Barcelona and its metropolitan area. It runs latin nights (reggaeton, bachata), Ladies Night and day parties, and brings you the best agenda in town.
Where are KuKo parties?
In Barcelona (Nix, in the Eixample, near Rocafort metro) and in El Prat de Llobregat (Terminal Zero).
What's the dress code?
Smart casual.
What's the age requirement?
Entry is for over 21s only.
How do I get to Terminal Zero (El Prat)?
For KuKo events we organise a taxi so you arrive with zero hassle.
How do I book or buy tickets?
By WhatsApp (bookings and guestlist) or through our online ticket agenda.
