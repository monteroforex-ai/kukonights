What night does KuKo run at Nix?

KuKo's latin night at Nix is on Saturdays. Check the agenda for each date and the opening hours.

Entry is for over 21s only. Bring your ID or passport.

Smart casual. Avoid very informal sportswear.

Yes, tables and bottle service are available subject to availability. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out.
